What is Bazooka Noodles?

Bazooka Noodles is a fast, cartoony worm arena game for Android, published by xBitsNL Gaming. Slither around a muddy arena, gobble up glowing food to grow longer, and make rival worms crash into your body. Then grab a crate, pull out a bazooka and blow your opponents down to size.

Features

Eat & grow

Gobble food to grow longer and climb the leaderboard. Boost for speed, at the cost of a little length.

Bazookas & banana bombs

Open mystery crates for a bazooka, bouncing grenades or a banana bomb that bursts into clusters.

Snip them short

Explosions cut worms in two: the tail bursts into food for you to collect. Small worms are blown away entirely.

150 skins

Tiger stripes, lava, galaxies, rainbows, crowns, viking helmets and DJ headphones. Earn coins by playing to unlock them.

9 languages

English, Dutch, German, French, Spanish, Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Hindi.

Play anywhere

Battle a full arena of computer-controlled worms, fully offline. No account, no sign-up.

How to play

  1. Steer with the joystick on the left half of the screen.
  2. Eat the glowing food to grow. Hold Boost to sprint.
  3. Trap rivals: if their head hits your body, they burst into food.
  4. Grab a crate and tap Fire to launch whatever is inside.
  5. Survive, top the leaderboard and spend your coins on new skins.

Get the game

Bazooka Noodles will be distributed through Google Play under the package name com.xbitsnl.bazookanoodles. A store link will be added here when the game is released.
